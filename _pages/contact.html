02 / Engagement formats

Four ways the work gets structured.

Pick the format that matches the shape of the problem — the technical surface area is the same either way.

01Project

Project pentest

Fixed-scope engagement against a defined target — web, Android, network, Active Directory, embedded systems. Kick-off → testing → debrief → final report.

02Retainer

Retainer / advisory

Monthly or quarterly recurring access. Useful when you need a security voice in design reviews, threat-model discussions, or pre-release sign-off without the overhead of a full project.

03Research

Bug-bounty & CVE

Coordinated vulnerability disclosure on your product, joint research on third-party software you depend on, or stewardship of an existing program with structural advice.

04Training

Training & workshops

Internal sessions on offensive tradecraft, secure coding, threat modeling, or incident response. Tailored to your engineering team's stack.
